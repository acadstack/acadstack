# AcadStack user guide

AcadStack is a web application for managing academics: courses, course offerings, enrolment, attendance, grades, fees, feedback, and doctoral (PhD) committees and progress reports. This guide explains how to use it day to day.

Setting AcadStack up for a university (roles, lists, grading scheme, sessions) is a separate job, covered in [Setting up AcadStack for your university](../university_setup.md).

## Who should read what

Everyone starts with chapter 1. Then read the chapter for your role. What you see in the menu bar depends on your role, so you may not have every screen this guide mentions. See [Roles and what they can see](05-reference.md#roles-and-what-they-can-see).

| If you are | Read |
|---|---|
| Anyone | [1. Getting started](01-getting-started.md) |
| A student (including PhD students) | [2. Students](02-students.md) |
| A faculty member or a batch advisor | [3. Faculty and advisors](03-faculty-and-advisors.md) |
| Academic office staff, head of department or dean | [4. Academic office, HOD and Dean](04-academic-office-hod-dean.md) |

## How this guide writes things

- A menu item is written as a path: *Courses → Courses Offered For Enrolment* means open the **Courses** menu in the top bar, then choose **Courses Offered For Enrolment**.
- Button and field names are in **bold**, exactly as they appear on screen.
- Each procedure is a numbered list. Notes under **What to know** cover rules that can surprise you.
- The screenshots use demo data. Your names, courses and dates will differ.

## Getting help

The **Help** link, last in the menu bar, shows who to contact at your university. Before you write to them, check [Troubleshooting](05-reference.md#troubleshooting) in this guide.
